About

SLEDGE

A Tekken 8 devotee whose five-character roster spans the whole game — the disciplined all-rounder (Jin), the radiant aggressor (Claudio), the relentless commando (Dragunov), the cerebral tactician (Victor), and the electrifying rebel (Lars).

Sledge mains Jin Kazama — aggressive and defensive in equal measure. Claudio Serafino backs him up as the straightforward aggressor. Sergei Dragunov and Victor Chevalier are the pairing that lives in the lab — hard to command, heavier to wield. Lars Alexandersson closes the set with speed and wall carry. No rank claims, no tier lists — this site is the roster, not a résumé.
